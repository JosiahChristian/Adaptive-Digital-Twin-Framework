# Experiment 141 — frozen ninth-population reconstruction

Experiment 141 generated 40 untouched seeds (44351–44390) only after the Experiment 142 action-only versus action-plus-context comparison, fixed-coverage rule, endpoints, bootstrap count, and success criteria were frozen.

The resulting population contained 10,254 action-context rows with 2,689 unsafe outcomes (26.224% prevalence), substantially higher than the preceding eighth-population prevalence. This therefore provided a meaningful distribution-shift challenge rather than a near-replication of the prior population.

Retrospective diagnostics showed weaker raw context separation than in Experiment 139, but these diagnostics did not alter the frozen prospective Experiment 142 analysis.

Artifacts use the suffix `351_390` under `results/`.

# Evidence Repository Policy

## Purpose

`main` is an experimental evidence record, not a finished paper.

Materials appropriate for `main` include:

- research questions and frozen experimental plans;
- preregistrations;
- experiment and audit code;
- generated data/result artifacts;
- seed definitions and unit-of-analysis records;
- negative and failed results;
- controls and falsification studies;
- provenance, hashes, workflow metadata, and reproducibility instructions;
- neutral evidence-status tables that distinguish supported, unresolved, falsified, and exploratory findings.

Materials that should not be presented on `main` as completed scholarly authorship include:

- manuscript-style Introduction, Discussion, Abstract, or Conclusion prose not written by the human author;
- publication-ready wording presented as the author's voice;
- literature-review prose or bibliography material created before the author has independently read and selected the sources for a paper;
- venue submission packages, author biographies, cover letters, or publication metadata before an author-written manuscript exists.

Machine-assisted audit and adjudication records may remain when they are clearly labeled as review records rather than author prose. Primary experimental artifacts remain authoritative when an audit summary and a producing artifact differ.

## Authorship boundary

A future paper may use this repository as its evidence base. The paper's substantive prose and interpretation must be authored by the human author. Machine assistance may be used later only under the applicable academic-integrity and publication rules and must not obscure who wrote or interpreted the work.

# ADT Venue Strategy — 2026-08-18

**Status:** publication-strategy record on the review branch. This does not alter scientific claims or experimental artifacts.

## Recommended submission sequence

### 1. Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory — PRIMARY TARGET

**Why it fits:** The manuscript is fundamentally about validity in a simulated decision pipeline, with explicit attention to experimental design, selection/comparison procedures, verification/validation, ranking effects, and uncertainty. The journal explicitly covers modelling and simulation methodology, experimental design, validation/verification, selection/comparison procedures, AI, embedded/intelligent systems, and engineering applications.

**Strengths for this manuscript:**
- strongest topical match to the current narrowed contribution;
- does not require claiming real-world deployment;
- manuscript can be framed around methodological validity in simulation rather than overstating digital-twin application maturity;
- hybrid publication model permits subscription publication without an author OA fee.

**Risk:** highly selective. The current published journal metrics report an acceptance rate around 6%, so the paper must make the methodological contribution unmistakable rather than reading as an internal post hoc audit of one project.

**Current publisher-reported OA APC:** approximately USD 3,110 excluding taxes; subscription route has no publication fee.

**Required packaging emphasis:**
1. modelling/simulation validity as the main contribution;
2. prospectively frozen controls and falsification chronology;
3. ranking-selection geometry as a general simulation-evaluation concern, while keeping the empirical result pipeline-specific;
4. timing validity as a second methodological case study;
5. reproducibility/provenance as a central strength.

### 2. IEEE Access — STRONG FALLBACK / PRAGMATIC TARGET

**Why it fits:** Broad IEEE scope, applications-oriented engineering research, and explicit willingness to publish technically correct negative results. The current manuscript's combination of adaptive decision pipelines, simulation, ranking, ML validity, and negative/falsification findings fits its multidisciplinary model.

**Strengths:**
- explicit negative-result compatibility;
- rapid binary review model;
- broader tolerance for interdisciplinary engineering framing;
- lower scope-mismatch risk than highly domain-specific digital-twin journals.

**Current publisher-reported APC:** USD 2,160 plus applicable taxes. Fully open access; no subscription/no-fee route.

**Publisher-reported review profile:** approximately 4-6 weeks submission-to-publication, with an average acceptance rate around 20%.

**Risk:** broad venue and mandatory APC; less methodologically specialized than the primary target.

### 3. Information and Software Technology — CONDITIONAL ALTERNATIVE

**Why it could fit:** Verification/validation, modelling, software quality, empirical studies, and a Short Communications track explicitly open to negative results.

**Why it is not first choice:** The current paper is not primarily about software-development practice. It would require a legitimate software-engineering framing around validation methodology rather than simply relabeling the current modelling study.

**Current publisher-reported OA APC:** approximately USD 3,890; subscription route has no publication fee.

### Reach-only targets not recommended first

**Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence:** high-visibility AI/engineering venue, but its current scope asks application papers to report real-world engineering use and public-data validation. The present simulation-only manuscript is at material desk-rejection risk unless new real-world evidence is added, which is not scientifically necessary for the current claim.

**Computers in Industry / Journal of Industrial Information Integration:** strong digital-twin/industrial visibility but application-oriented toward industrial integration or industrial ICT. The current paper lacks a genuine industrial deployment/use-case validation and should not be cosmetically reframed to imitate one.

**Decision Support Systems:** conceptually compatible with decision-system evaluation, but the manuscript's core contribution is more directly simulation/validation methodological than information-systems decision support.

## Decision

Prepare the current ADT manuscript first for **Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory**. Preserve **IEEE Access** as the predeclared fallback if the first venue rejects on novelty/scope rather than identifying a correctable scientific defect.

A venue rejection must not trigger claim inflation or a new experiment automatically. Reviewer criticism should be triaged into: (a) documentation/clarity; (b) analysis that can be done on existing evidence; or (c) genuinely new scientific question requiring new data.

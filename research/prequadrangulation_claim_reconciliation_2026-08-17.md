## Experiment 166 — reconciled claim map

### Claim A — clean-versus-poison membership changes localize near the clean fixed-budget cutoff

**Status: SUPPORTED as an empirical phenomenon.**

The existing-artifact audit, using generation seed as the inferential unit, found mean near-cutoff switch rate 0.15342 versus far-cutoff rate 0.01719, with mean near-minus-far difference 0.13623 and seed-bootstrap 95% interval [0.11424, 0.15789]. This survives the unit-of-analysis objection.

### Claim B — Criterion 2 independently supports a composition-to-decision mechanism

**Status: NOT SUPPORTED.**

The bookkeeping-preserving permutation null reproduced the observed strong negative correlation. The observed Spearman rho (-0.87318) was ordinary under the null (one-sided probability 0.53315). Criterion 2 therefore cannot be treated as independent mechanistic evidence.

### Claim C — near-cutoff membership switches are preferentially responsible for downstream selected-action changes

**Status: FALSIFIED by the frozen specificity analysis.**

Selected-action-change rate was 0.63871 for near-only switched contexts and 0.95425 for far-only switched contexts; the near-minus-far difference was -0.31554 with seed-bootstrap 95% interval [-0.40818, -0.23161]. The observed direction is opposite the proposed downstream-specificity interpretation.

### Claim D — the surviving near-cutoff localization is specific to poisoning

**Status: SPECIFICITY UNRESOLVED / NOT ESTABLISHED.**

The first prospectively frozen clean-label control was preserved as a failed-control result because it did not match perturbation magnitude. The separately frozen stronger label-preserving control subsequently achieved adequate matching to the historical poison perturbation. Under that matched comparison, poison and non-poison localization were essentially the same: poison mean near-minus-far enrichment 0.13623 versus matched control 0.13438; paired difference 0.001845; seed-bootstrap 95% interval [0.0000, 0.00554]. Because the frozen poisoning-specificity rule required the lower confidence bound to exclude zero, poisoning-specific localization is not supported.

### Claim E — Experiment 166 establishes a local cutoff-geometry poisoning mechanism

**Status: NOT SUPPORTED by the combined audited evidence.**

The historical preregistered pass remains part of the scientific record, but the later audit shows that the conjunction of its criteria was not sufficient to diagnose the mechanism originally named. The defensible statement is narrower: a robust near-cutoff localization phenomenon occurs under the tested perturbation, but current evidence does not show that it is poisoning-specific or that near-cutoff switches preferentially drive downstream selected-action changes.

## Global manuscript boundary

The ADT evidence currently supports bounded simulator-internal empirical phenomena, falsification results, and exploratory pre-decision signal. It does not support claims that the adaptive-digital-twin problem has been solved, that poisoning-specific cutoff mechanics have been established, that the leaked harmful-expansion model predicts prospectively, or that findings are deployment-ready or cross-domain validated.

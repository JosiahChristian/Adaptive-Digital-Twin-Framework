# Final Quadrangulation Gate

This document defines the required closing review process before ADT results are treated as manuscript-grade conclusions.

## Purpose

The research program uses multiple internal safeguards—preregistration, held-out populations, prospective replication, negative-result retention, claim ledgers, and reproducibility artifacts. Those controls reduce self-confirmation but do not replace independent adversarial review.

The final claim set therefore requires a four-part quadrangulation process after the active experiment sequence and manuscript package are sufficiently stable.

## Four review perspectives

### 1. Experiment lane

The experiment lane must provide the canonical record of what was actually frozen, executed, replicated, falsified, and left unresolved. It owns no special authority over interpretation; its role is factual experimental provenance.

Required inputs include:

- preregistrations and frozen analysis plans;
- exact result artifacts;
- seed/population definitions;
- negative and null results;
- workflow/reproducibility status;
- deviations, if any, from preregistered procedures.

### 2. Evidence-synthesis lane

The synthesis lane must map experimental evidence to the narrowest defensible claims, including contradictory and failed results.

It must explicitly separate:

- supported observations;
- partial results;
- failed replications;
- falsified hypotheses;
- unresolved mechanism questions;
- prohibited interpretations.

### 3. Independent external adversarial reviewer

A separate platform/agent should review the frozen repository without being told which result the authors hope to preserve.

Suggested instruction:

> Try to reject this work. Identify every claim not supported by the evidence, every plausible alternative explanation, leakage or post-hoc risk, statistical weakness, missing baseline, replication weakness, and generalization overreach. Distinguish fatal flaws, correctable weaknesses, and optional improvements. Treat favorable metrics as untrusted until their provenance and evaluation design justify them.

The external reviewer should receive the repository, current research question, manuscript draft, claim ledger, and reproducibility map, but not a persuasive summary designed to steer its conclusion.

### 4. Evidence-based reconciliation

The final pass compares the experiment record, synthesis assessment, and external critique against the actual artifacts.

Disagreements are not resolved by majority vote. Each criticism is classified as:

- **supported and unresolved** — requires new experiment, analysis, or narrower claim;
- **supported and correctable** — requires documentation/statistical/reporting repair;
- **already addressed** — artifact and location are cited;
- **not supported by the record** — rebuttal is tied to concrete evidence;
- **optional extension** — scientifically useful but not necessary for the bounded claim.

## Freeze condition before quadrangulation

Quadrangulation should begin only when:

- no active preregistered experiment needed for the central claim is still running;
- canonical result tables and chronology are synchronized;
- the manuscript draft reflects current negative and replication results;
- reproducibility instructions identify exact inputs and outputs;
- claim and falsification ledgers are current;
- no known material contradiction exists between README, manuscript, and result artifacts.

## ADT-specific review targets

The external attack should pay particular attention to:

1. temporal leakage in pre-decision harmful-expansion features;
2. whether generated populations are genuinely independent enough for claimed replication;
3. harm-label sensitivity and event-selection dependence;
4. whether strong pooled metrics hide action/block confounding;
5. whether the Experiment 166 cutoff-localization result depends critically on the fixed intervention budget or cutoff-band definition;
6. multiplicity and hypothesis-generation risk across the long experimental chronology;
7. whether the decision-aware line is being allowed to overtake the stronger pre-decision publication candidate without sufficient replication;
8. whether any simulator-specific result is phrased as real-world cyber-physical or biomedical safety evidence.

## Promotion rule

A manuscript claim is promoted only after all material external criticisms are either resolved by evidence, repaired, or reflected in a narrower claim. Unresolved fatal or central-design criticisms block manuscript-grade status.

Quadrangulation is therefore a required evidence gate, not a ceremonial final review.

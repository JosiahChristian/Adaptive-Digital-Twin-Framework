# Research Questions

## Primary Research Question

Can adaptive digital twins learn the behavior of complex dynamical systems while maintaining consistency with the underlying physical processes governing those systems?


## Secondary Research Questions

### 1. System Representation

How can complex evolving systems be represented mathematically in a form suitable for simulation, prediction, and control?


### 2. Predictive Modeling

Can machine learning models accurately predict future system states from incomplete or noisy observations?


### 3. Physics Integration

How can physical laws and mathematical constraints be incorporated into learning systems to improve prediction accuracy and reliability?


### 4. Adaptive Learning

Can a computational model continuously update its understanding of a system as new observations become available?


### 5. Control and Optimization

Can predictions from an adaptive digital twin be used to determine optimal actions for improving system performance?


## Long-Term Research Vision

Develop computational frameworks capable of modeling, predicting, and controlling complex evolving systems through the integration of numerical simulation, artificial intelligence, and physics-informed learning.
